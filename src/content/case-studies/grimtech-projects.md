## The starting point

Grimtech Projects is a ground-engineering and deep-foundation contractor: piling, diaphragm walls, pile
testing, ground improvement and equipment hire. Its buyers are structural engineers, project managers and
contractors — people who search in precise technical language and who are researching long before anyone
requests a quote.

This is the opposite of a local-services SEO problem. Nobody types "piling near me". They type "high
strain dynamic load test in India", and they expect the result to be written by someone who has actually
done one.

## What we did

The site was structured around **how engineers search, not how the company is organised**. Each service
got its own page — bored piles, micro piles, sheet pile installation, diaphragm walls, jet grouting,
pile testing — rather than one "Services" page listing everything.

Alongside that, technical guides for the research stage: D-wall construction, deep soil mixing, stone
column ground improvement, vibro float. These are the searches that happen months before a tender, and
ranking for them is how a specialist contractor gets onto a shortlist.

Equipment hire was treated as a separate funnel entirely. "Vibro hammer on rent" is a different buyer
with a different timeline, competing largely against IndiaMART listings.

## What happened

**Five position-1 rankings**, including the two most commercially valuable in the set:

- "Piling Contractor in Delhi" — **1**
- "Deep Foundation Solutions (India)" — **1**
- "Sheet Pile Installation Price" — **1**
- "High Strain Dynamic Load Test in India" — **1**
- "vibro float in india" — **1**

Across the tracked list, **25 of 33 queries sit in the top 10 and 15 in the top 3**. The technical guides
paid off: "stone column" at 2, "impact hammer in India" at 3, "jet grouting" at 6.

Eight queries also show an **AI Overview** appearance in the supplied tracker — Google's generated answer
citing the site. That is worth noting and worth being careful about, which we cover below.

## Where it is weak

Broad single-term searches are the soft spot: "d wall" at 19, "ground improvement" at 20, "spun pile
contractor" at 22, "Diaphragm Wall Construction in India" at 31, and both "Pile Testing Solutions" and
"Non-destructive Testing" reported only as ">20". Several of these are terms where the page that ranks
is not the page that should — "Diaphragm Wall Construction in India" points at the generic piling
contractor URL rather than the diaphragm wall page, which is a fixable internal-linking problem.

## What this does not prove — and this one matters

This case study is **visibility only**. Unlike the other clients here, no Search Console data, no enquiry
log and no won-project values were supplied for Grimtech. We cannot tell you what these rankings earned,
and we are not going to imply it.

Two further cautions. The positions come from a user-supplied list with no measurement date, and an
earlier sheet for the same site reports different numbers by a different method — so treat these as a
snapshot, not a controlled measurement. And the AI Overview column records a "Yes" with no screenshot,
citation or date behind it: it indicates reported visibility, not a ranking position, and nothing in the
files shows it produced traffic.
