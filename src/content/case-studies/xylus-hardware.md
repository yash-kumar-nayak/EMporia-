## The starting point

Xylus Hardware Products is a hardware wholesaler at Loha Mandi, on Bulandshahr Road Industrial Area in
Ghaziabad. Its customers are contractors, furniture makers and trade buyers who need a specific fitting
today and will drive to whoever has it.

That customer does not browse. They search, they check the map, they go. Which makes the Google Business
Profile the entire shopfront.

## What the numbers show

**321 of 375 recorded actions — 86% — were direction requests.** Calls were 46. Website clicks were
**eight**, across six months.

Eight. That number is worth sitting with, because it is genuinely useful: for this business, the website
is close to irrelevant to the buying decision. Anyone advising Xylus to invest in a website redesign
would be spending money in the wrong place. The profile, the photos, the address accuracy and the stock
information are where the returns are.

## The pattern in the rankings

The supplied list puts Xylus in the **Maps top 10 for 23 of 30 searches, and in the top 3 for four**. But
the interesting part is *which* ones:

Searches that name the market rank at the top — "hardware store near Central Bank Loha Mandi" at **1**,
"hardware shop in Bulandshahr Road Industrial Area" at **2**, "industrial hardware in Loha Mandi" at
**3**. Product-specific trade terms follow: bed fittings and sofa legs at 4, digital locks at 5.

Generic city-wide searches are the gap. "Hardware store in ghaziabad", "hardware shop in ghaziabad" and
"best hardware store in ghaziabad" all sit outside the top 10 — and those are the highest-volume terms
in the set. Also missing: "building material and hardware store ghaziabad", "furniture hardware in
ghaziabad", "door hardware and fittings in ghaziabad" and Sahibabad.

We are showing the 23 wins and the 7 losses in the same list, because a hardware buyer who reads only the
wins has been sold to, not informed.

## What this does not prove

The rankings came to us as a list, not a dated export, and without SERP screenshots, device or map pin.
Maps positions shift with the searcher's location, so these should be re-checked before anyone treats
them as live. The GBP totals are real and screenshot-verified; the sales they produced are not measured,
because no till or invoice data was supplied.
