## The starting point

Loan4Wealth is a loan agency in Raj Nagar, Ghaziabad, competing against banks, national aggregators and
Justdial for people searching "personal loan in Ghaziabad" on their phone. It is a high-intent, high-
competition category where the searcher usually calls the first credible result.

## What the numbers show

In six months the profile recorded **624 interactions**, and the split is unusually informative:

- **329 call taps** — 53% of everything
- **239 direction requests**
- **56 website clicks**

More than half of all activity was someone tapping *call*. For a loan agency that is the conversion
event. Compare it with The Pelican Hotel, where directions dominated: the same Google surface produces
completely different behaviour depending on what the business sells, and it tells you where to invest.

## The uncomfortable part: Maps is winning, the website is not

The hyper-local terms perform. "Loan agency in Raj Nagar", "personal loan in RDC Raj Nagar" and
"business loan in Raj Nagar" all sit at **Maps position 2**. Across the snapshot, 17 of 29 complete rows
have a numerical top-10 Maps position.

The website is a different story. Of those same 29 rows, **exactly one** website position falls inside
the top 10 — "business od loan in ghaziabad" at organic position 9. Everything else is outside.

There is one result that could easily be mis-sold: the Raj Nagar organic "#2" in the supplied data
belongs to **Justdial**, not to loan4wealth.com. It is not this client's ranking, and we are not counting
it as one.

Whole clusters are still absent: the entire home-loan group, car loans, education loans, MSME and working
capital all sit outside the top 10 on both surfaces. That is the roadmap, and it is why this case study
is a progress report rather than a victory lap.

## What this does not prove

The ranking snapshot has no date, no device and no map pin, and Maps results move depending on where the
searcher physically is. The GBP totals come from separate screenshots with no earlier period to compare
against, so no growth percentage is claimed. Nothing here links any specific search to any specific call,
and no disbursal or revenue figures were supplied.
