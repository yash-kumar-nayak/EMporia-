## The starting point

IPEM Group of Institutions is a multi-programme college in Ghaziabad running BBA, BCA, B.Com, B.Ed, MBA,
MCA and a full law stream. In January 2026 its CRM recorded **37 enquiries** from the "Organic Web"
source — a college with eleven programmes getting roughly one organic enquiry a day.

The competition for "MBA college in Ghaziabad" is not other colleges' brochures. It is aggregator sites —
Shiksha, Collegedunia, Justdial — which outrank individual institutions for almost every generic course
query. A college that does not rank for its own programme names is invisible at exactly the moment a
student is choosing.

## What we did

The work was structural, not cosmetic. Prospective students search by **programme plus city** — "BCA
college in Ghaziabad" — and the site had no page that answered that query directly. Course information
lived inside a downloadable prospectus, which Google cannot rank and a student on a phone will not open.

We built one indexable landing page per programme, each carrying fees, eligibility, duration and a
visible enquiry form, then concentrated internal linking on the programmes with the shortest decision
window ahead of the July intake.

## What happened

Enquiries compounded month over month: 37, 58, 74, 98, 189, 318, then **566 in July**. August settled to
346, which matches the admissions calendar rather than a drop in visibility — July is the peak intake
month for these programmes.

Across the eight months, the CRM attributed **100 admissions** to organic web — a 5.9% enquiry-to-
admission rate. B.Ed was the standout, converting 32 admissions from 255 enquiries. BBA drew almost the
same enquiry volume (263) but converted only 9, which tells the college something useful about where the
gap is: the traffic arrived, the programme page did its job, and the conversation after the enquiry is
where that programme loses people.

By August, **31 of 38 tracked keywords sat in the top 10 and 17 in the top 3**. Six B.Sc Digital
Marketing queries entered at positions 1 and 2 — a programme with no prior organic footprint at all.

## Where it went backwards

Two MBA queries moved the wrong way: "MBA Institute in Ghaziabad" fell from 22 to 38, and "Private MBA
College in Ghaziabad" from 25 to 36. Both were already outside the top 20, where rankings are volatile
and traffic is negligible — but they are in the tracker and they are in this case study, because a
report that only shows the green arrows is a sales document, not evidence.

## What this does not prove

The honest boundary of this case: "Organic Web" is IPEM's own CRM label. We did not audit how each
enquiry was tagged, and nothing in the supplied files links a specific keyword to a specific admission.
Read the ranking chart and the enquiry chart as two things that happened in the same period — not as one
causing the other.
